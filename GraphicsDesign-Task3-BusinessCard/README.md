# Task 3 – Business Card Design

**Internship:** Oasis Infobyte  
**Track:** Graphic Design  
**Brand:** NEXORA

## Project Description
Designed a professional double-sided business card for NEXORA, a fictional technology company.

- Back side: Phone, email, website and social media.
- Consistent brand colours and typography.
- Print-ready layout with 3 mm bleed.

## Deliverables
- Front and back business card designs
